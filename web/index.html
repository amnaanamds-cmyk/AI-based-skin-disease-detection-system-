Upload a close-up photo of a mole or lesion. DermaAI checks image quality, estimates the likelihood of seven common conditions, shows where it looked, and tells you how soon to see a dermatologist.

Tips for a good photo
  • Use daylight; avoid flash and shadows.
  • Fill most of the frame with the lesion and a little surrounding skin.
  • Hold steady and tap to focus. Retake if blurry.

How it works

1

Quality gate

Sharpness, exposure, glare and framing are measured. Poor photos are rejected with instructions instead of a guess.

2

Deep learning

An EfficientNet CNN fused with age, sex and body site, trained on dermoscopic images with lesion-grouped splits and class balancing.

3

Calibration & uncertainty

Temperature scaling makes probabilities honest; test-time augmentation and entropy flag images the model is unsure about.

4

Explainability

Grad-CAM shows where the network looked; an independent classical ABCDE analysis gives a second, interpretable opinion.

5

Safety-first triage

Thresholds favour melanoma sensitivity: it is better to over-refer than to miss a cancer.

6

Skin care & products

Facial analysis measures redness, blemishes, tone, texture and shine, then a rules engine builds a routine and matches product types.

7

Progress & assistant

Before/after comparison tracks change in a spot, and the assistant answers questions with built-in red-flag escalation.
